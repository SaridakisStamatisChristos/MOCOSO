## Constructive reduction challenges

1. **Generic equality-resource pair DAG:** vertices (i,j), arcs (i,j)→(j,k) with a[k]>2a[j]-a[i]. On superconvex arrays, there are Theta(p³) valid arcs, so generic O(Eh) resource DP is cubic-in-p times budget, not p² times budget. Turner Theorem 2 alone cannot directly give the G3 target.
2. **Event-sweep length-only:** Kluev's ordering plus omission-gap resource augmentation yields O(p²(h+1)) transitions and O(p(h+1)+p²) working words for *length*, but its cited statement does not provide the global lex-min entire witness with those bounds. PR #11 makes this combination explicit, not novel as a primitive.
3. **G3 reverse-state lex:** G3 proves a threshold-monotone reverse recurrence F(i,j,r) with smallest-next-index tie. It derives cubic-time full lex at cubic space by additional argument. None of the cited earlier statements is demonstrated to imply this exact combination without extra lemmas.
4. **G7 omitted-list Pareto:** further add bounded-count distinct-part omission representation and lex order reversal; this is G7's proved composition, **not attributed to previous sources as a verbatim theorem**.
5. **Cubic/quadratic full lex:** neither a prior direct theorem nor a new valid G7 derivation is established. OPEN.
